# How chains get their grades

`Skip Slop/Data/SeedChains.json` is generated by `scripts/build_chains.py`. Every entry has a `ratingBasis`:

- **`curated`** — graded by hand (`scripts/data/curated_chains.json`, 186 chains). The generator copies these grades unchanged and checks that none changed. The app shows them as **Known chain**.
- **`category`** — a chain we know exists but haven't graded. Its grade comes only from the rules below. The app shows it as **Chain, estimated grade**, with the dashed "Estimated" placard.

No per-chain fact (distributor, sourcing, quality) is ever invented for a `category` entry. Their `distributor` and `parentCompany` are always empty, and the detail screen says the grade comes from the kind of place it is.

## Sources

| Source | What it provides | Pinned version |
|---|---|---|
| Curated list | Name, grade, category, distributor, citation | in repo |
| [OpenStreetMap name-suggestion-index](https://github.com/osmlab/name-suggestion-index) (`data/brands/amenity/{fast_food,restaurant,cafe,ice_cream,bar,pub}.json`, `data/brands/shop/{bakery,pastry,coffee,deli}.json`) | Brand name, aliases (`matchNames`, `brand`, `official_name`, …), Wikidata ID, `amenity`/`shop`/`cuisine` tags | commit `721a3b9b` (2026-10-11) |
| [Wikipedia: List of restaurant chains in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?oldid=1374357555) | Chains NSI lacks, and the section each is listed under | oldid 1374357555 (2026-09-11) |

QSR / Technomic Top 500 lists are paywalled, so they aren't used.

NSI brands are kept when their `locationSet` includes the US: `us`, a US state (`us-*.geojson`), Northern America (`021`), the Americas (`019`), or the world (`001`), and doesn't exclude `us`. Brands whose names have no Latin letters are skipped, since US MapKit results use Latin names.

## Merging

1. Curated entries go in first and keep their slug and grade.
2. An NSI brand merges into an existing entry when its Wikidata ID, its name, or any of its aliases matches a curated name or alias. Merged brands add their Wikidata ID and aliases only.
3. Remaining NSI brands become `category` entries.
4. Wikipedia rows merge the same way (Wikidata ID via the Wikipedia API, or name). Unmatched rows become `category` entries. The "Defunct" section is skipped.
5. Names and aliases that are only generic words ("Pizza", "Cafe", "Burger") are dropped. An alias claimed by two different chains is dropped from both.

## Category rules

| Format (from NSI tags or Wikipedia section) | `category` | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| `amenity=fast_food`, `shop=deli`; Wikipedia quick-service sections (Burgers, Chicken, Pizza/Italian, Sandwiches, Mexican, Asian, …) | `fast_food` | **N/A** (`grey`) |
| `amenity=cafe`, `shop=coffee`; Wikipedia "Beverages" | `coffee` | **N/A** (`grey`) |
| `amenity=ice_cream`; Wikipedia "Frozen desserts" | `dessert` | **N/A** (`grey`) |
| `shop=bakery`, `shop=pastry`; Wikipedia "Baked goods" | `bakery` | **N/A** (`grey`) |
| `amenity=restaurant`; Wikipedia "Casual dining", "Arcade and game centers" | `casual_dining` | **C** (`orange`) |
| `amenity=bar`, `amenity=pub` | `bar` | **C** (`orange`) |

If NSI and Wikipedia disagree on format, NSI wins, since it's per brand rather than per list section.

### Why these grades

The rules reuse grades the app already gives:

- **Quick service → N/A.** All 46 curated fast-food and coffee chains are N/A, and the app's rating scale says "Fast food. It is what it is, so we don't grade it." `RatingInferenceEngine` does the same for fast-food and coffee places it can't match.
- **Sit-down chain → C.** C is what `RatingInferenceEngine` gives an unverified chain-style restaurant ("Chain-style name pattern — unverified"). Most curated sit-down chains are F (57 of 78 casual-dining chains), but F and F− say a chain is supplied by Sysco or US Foods. That's a factual claim we can't make without a source, so the rules never assign F, F−, A, or A+.

A category grade should be replaced by a curated one, with a citation, once a chain has been researched. Add it to `scripts/data/curated_chains.json` and rerun the script.

## Rebuilding

```bash
python3 scripts/build_chains.py            # pinned sources
python3 scripts/build_chains.py --nsi-ref main --wiki-oldid <newer revid>   # refresh
```

The script writes `Skip Slop/Data/SeedChains.json` and `docs/data/chains_build_report.md` (counts by provenance, grade, and category). It fails if any curated grade would change.
